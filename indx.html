Meet the Doctor

Dr. Aanya Sharma

Senior Consultant — Internal Medicine & Cardiology

With over 15 years of clinical experience across leading hospitals in India and the UK, Dr. Sharma brings a rare combination of global expertise and genuine compassion. She believes that great medicine begins with listening — truly listening — to her patients.

A graduate of AIIMS New Delhi and fellowship holder from the Royal College of Surgeons, Edinburgh, she has published over 30 peer-reviewed papers and spoken at international medical conferences across four continents.
